# Atlas asset credits

The following models are distributed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Textures were resized for web delivery. The Earth and Mars illustrations were rendered from these textures. The astronaut model was also converted to metallic/roughness materials and compressed with Meshopt.

| Moon | AD&D 4D, [La Luna / Viaje Virtual Guiado](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/la-luna-viaje-virtual-guiado-3d-vr-f2cdadece5b54fadaddd80e881e4b848) |

Atlas creates the spacecraft silhouettes, star field, and audio in code or SVG. The spacecraft are illustrative and do not represent actual mission hardware.

The older prototypes and their assets remain in Git history under their original terms. The licenses above apply to the listed assets only. See the dependency license files in the production build for Three.js and Meshoptimizer.
